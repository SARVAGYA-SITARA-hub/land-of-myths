The World of 2034

Status: PROPOSED — only what the two chapters show.

What the text establishes

Date: 14 August 2034. A Thursday, though the text does not say so.

Technology: the only future-facing object on the page is a smartphone — "the screen said", a music playlist, air pods. Everything else is ordinary: gas cylinder, steel cup, tea strainer, plastic chair, sliding glass door, steel guard railings, a minivan collecting waste. The year is not doing much yet.

Place: an Indian setting. Names are Lalbagh, Malipur, Janaknagar. Whether these are real, renamed, or invented is UNRESOLVED.

Weather: a monsoon-adjacent morning. Cloudy, threatening rain, the smell of soil, cold wind, wet grass, dew on the wooden and steel benches.

The house, as catalogued

The draft spends real space on this, so it matters:

  • Upstairs: his bedroom (double bed, polished brown wood, wrinkled sheet, dark

blue walls) and one door opposite his own that stays closed.

  • Stairs: down to marble floor, "invading the drowsiness".
  • Ground floor: total silence. Light switch; kitchen immediately beside it.
  • Kitchen: an L-shaped platform along two walls, shelves below, two empty

compartments meant for a gas cylinder or storage.

  • Next room: drawing room, large sliding glass door onto the front yard garden.
  • Bathroom: far end of the wall sheltering kitchen and drawing room.
  • Entrance: two doors — an old western red cedar door older than the boy, and

outside it a thick carbon steel gate.

  • Boundary: red brick wall with a box cut into it reading 'LETTER BOX'.

The thing the world is missing

Nothing supernatural has happened in two chapters. If this book has a speculative element, it has not appeared yet. Do not assume one, and do not invent one.
