A Letter to God

A novel by Sarvagya Sitara

On the morning of 14 August 2034 a boy named Nakshatra makes tea by habit, waits a minute at a door that never opens, and stops at the letterbox with his hand half raised. At school they call his name twice and he does not answer. It has been two years since the thing the book will not name.
