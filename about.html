About

Sarvagya
Sitara

Writing four serials that share nothing but a question: what does a person become when a system hands them power they did not earn?

The work

Four serials, each with its own cosmology. They are deliberately kept apart — no shared universe, no crossover, no reading order beyond what you feel like today. A power system in one book is not a rule in another.

Everything here is published as it is written. Chapters appear when they are ready, and the Start here page is the honest map of what exists today.

How it is made

Each book is written beside its own world-bible — the private document where names, dates and rules are kept straight. The lore pages here are drawn from those bibles, which is why they read like working notes in places. They are working notes, cleaned up enough to publish.
