The collection

77 chapters · 114,837 words · 46 entries

These are four separate worlds. They do not share a cosmology, a continent, a power system or a single character. Keeping them apart is the whole job.

Chronicles of a Transmigrator in Parallel Universe is serialising on Royal Road. Arin carries a system that treats belief as fuel: rumour becomes claim, justification is the cost, and fragmentation is the price. Eldoris is its first gate.

Against the Heavens is in draft. Ajax Wilhelmshire takes the heavens by force and corrupts whatever he grasps. Zhen Shuri reads the debts the world owes and collects on necessity — he never hits harder, he only sees further.

Killer Petals is in draft. Santino is killed for a throne he never wanted and wakes on the altar with a rose drinking his blood, and every death it feeds on turns another petal red.

A Letter to God is in draft. On 14 August 2034 a boy makes tea by habit, waits at a door that never opens, and stops at the letterbox with his hand half raised. Two years since the thing the book will not name.
