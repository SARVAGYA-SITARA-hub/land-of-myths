Ajax Wilhelmshire

Status: main protagonist. POV of ch1–4, 6, 8–13.

Confirmed

  • Eldest son of Governor Wilhelmshire; family is one of the republic's most prominent
  • Seventeen. Turned seventeen three months before ch1
  • Lower body completely paralysed from an accident; uses a wheelchair. The accident is remembered for "a kind of pain that made one forget how to scream"
  • Thick silky black hair, black eyes. Looks noble and regal rather than handsome
  • Before the accident: lively, optimistic, enjoyed conversation, dreamed of exploring the skies
  • After: friends stopped visiting, letters thinned. He read more and studied more, hoping that if he became interesting enough they would return. They did not
  • At the banquet he was already changed — patient, dry, watchful
  • Strengths: acute observational eye (fixes the flower arrangement, the wiring, the tablecloth, spots the painted vase mark); remembers names; dry humour; reads people accurately
  • Weaknesses: not flexible; bad at social gatherings; almost no friends; overly curious; does things his own way; loves to experiment
  • Emotionally he is "a volcano which erupts immediately when full" — unlike Zhen, who shows emotion gradually
  • Hobby: poetry. Enjoys reading and writing it
  • Asked the stars: "What had he done to deserve such a fate? What crime had earned him this punishment?" and decided that whatever the price, he would pay it to stand again — "even if he had to use force"

What he can do (as of ch15)

Perception only. Four weeks of practice. He can recognise the internal network of "threads" even during ordinary activity, and he can feel where his Qi is thin. That is all.

He has not created Demonic Qi. He has not attempted the scripture's method — and Cheonma never wrote down how. See The Demonic Path.

Briefly, twice, an act of deliberate selfhood (holding his own name, then a specific memory of morning light) made the parasite pause. He does not know what that was, whether it helped, or whether it was real. It was instinct, not technique — and it is the same instinct the hijack will require.

Physical description (from the original sheet)

Thick silky black hair and black eyes. He looks noble and regal rather than handsome.

According to secret letters his sister exchanges with her friends, "if his brother wasn't disabled he would definitely be on top of the 'Ideal Men list to Snatch' list" — a secret ranking chart published every year in women's circles, famous across the continent, governed by an unspoken rule that men must never be told about it.

This is a useful device. It gives you a running joke (Ajax is the last to know), a way to show the sister's world, and a way for strangers to have opinions about him before he enters a room.

Inheritance

Cheonma's diary → the Reverse Heaven Divine Scripture.

Crucially: he does not know what the scripture really is. He reads it as a philosophical curiosity and an interesting question. He has not attempted the method — and Cheonma himself wrote "I have not understood the method. I have only confirmed the possibility."

He does not know that success would mean stealing from heaven. This must be the engine of the volume: he is being recruited into a crime he does not know is a crime.

  • Infected with the parasite — see The Six Calamities, Calamity I. Fever, blood on the handkerchief, pressure behind the ribs. Has felt something "answer" him twice.

Open questions

  • His age relative to the accident: how long ago? UNRESOLVED
  • Does he suspect the diary is dangerous? He has noticed the dreams are consistent and give new information each time, which he finds hard to dismiss. He trusts it. That trust is the vulnerability.
