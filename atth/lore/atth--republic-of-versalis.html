Republic of Versalis

Status: PROPOSED name. The political entity containing the Wilhelmshire estate and the Central Kingdom. See 02 Open Decisions — the manuscript never names the republic.

Confirmed political structure (ch2)

  • The country has a royal family, and the crown is the highest authority
  • Governors are appointed to oversee major administrative territories: managing affairs, maintaining institutions, implementing royal policy
  • Five governors in total
  • They are not nobles in the traditional sense. They do not own the lands they administer and cannot treat territories as personal inheritance
  • Their authority comes from the crown; responsibilities extend only as far as the royal administration permits
  • "They were, in a sense, limbs of the royal government. A healthy kingdom needed functioning limbs. But they were still limbs." ← the best line in ch2 and a load-bearing idea

How to deepen it without breaking it

The Babylonian model slots in underneath what ch2 already says, and makes the "limbs" line devastating:

  • The governors' authority is a renewable lease, not a grant. Renewed annually. That is why the Wilhelmshires host an enormous reception — lease renewal is social, not administrative.
  • Officially the land belongs to a god, held in trust. The crown is the god's tenant-in-chief; the governors are sub-tenants.
  • The five governorships correspond to five treaty cities, each with its own patron deity, making the "republic" a treaty rather than a state.
  • This converts your existing republic from generic-European into something specific and strange, without changing a single fact established in ch2.

Status: PROPOSED. Do not treat as canon until the author confirms.

The Central Kingdom (CONFIRMED as a place, ch7)

Zhen's family relocates there for work. It has "more workshops and industrial facilities," higher demand for magical engineering, more competition, and higher cost of living. The academy is there.

Note the geography problem: the Central Kingdom is both where Zhen wants to go for opportunity and where the academy is. That may mean Ajax's school and Zhen's destination are the same place. UNRESOLVED — the author should decide whether they meet there in volume 1.
