Zhen Shuri

Status: main protagonist. POV of ch5, ch7.

Confirmed

  • Seventeen. Commoner. Family is nomadic — they move between towns taking menial work
  • Father repairs household devices; mother does cleaning and cooking; has a younger sibling
  • Long brown hair, green eyes. In ch1 he is working as household staff at the Wilhelmshire estate, collar slightly crooked
  • A transmigrator from Earth who has memories of the original Zhen Shuri
  • Engineer. Designs a compact magical regulator — takes unstable energy and distributes it through smaller channels. The problem he is solving is cost: existing regulators are bulky or need rare components
  • Cannot afford the academy. His father tells him to keep his job and find a workshop apprenticeship instead
  • Persuaded his family to relocate to the Central Kingdom for work and opportunity

Personality

  • Creative, imaginative, executes before his mind reaches a conclusion
  • Plans fairly well; patience is a weakness
  • Will compromise on small things for a greater good
  • Lazy when at ease; avoids tiring work
  • Does not do much experimentation — unlike Ajax
  • Very talented and cunning; "basically the great con artist"
  • Capable when he commits. Quite emotional

Voice

Dry, deflecting, self-aware. Defends himself with jokes — "And it worked for nearly four seconds." / "Three." / "Four, if we count the moment before it exploded." Genuinely funny in a way Ajax is not: Ajax's humour is observational, Zhen's is verbal.

What he knows (CONFIRMED, ch7)

  • Six calamities will come
  • The first is a sentient, hive-minded, fast-evolving parasite created by Heaven
  • It arrives roughly a month after "the beginning of the original sequence"
  • Ajax Wilhelmshire is the first to contract it
  • He does not know how it spreads, whether it can be detected before symptoms, or whether his memories are reliable
  • His memories are "fragments," and he knows fragments are dangerous

The mechanic that should carry his arc

He is holding a copy of the world's ending — but it is the version before Ajax changed anything. Every act of defiance by Ajax makes Zhen's knowledge slightly more wrong, and Zhen will not understand why. That is his volume 1 mystery, and it welds the two plotlines together. See The Two Heavens.

Physical description (from the original sheet)

Long brown hair, green eyes. Seventeen.

Inheritance

Merlin's tome. First lesson: Perception. See Qi and the Cycle.

Open questions

  • How much of the original novel does he actually remember, in what form — read it, watched it, wrote it? UNRESOLVED, and important. It determines how reliable his information is and whether he can be surprised.
  • Does he have any cultivation ability yet? No — CONFIRMED. He has done perception exercises only.
