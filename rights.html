Ownership

Copyright in all text, artwork and design on this site is held by Sarvagya Sitara. It exists from the moment each work was written or drawn, under the Copyright Act, 1957 (India); registration is a record of that right, not the source of it.

The peacock-feather sigil that marks this site is an original drawing and the identifying mark of this body of work. It is the thing most likely to be copied, so it is stated here plainly: it is not clip art, not stock, and not available for reuse as a logo, avatar, watermark or brand.

What you may do — no permission needed

Read. Link to any page. Quote a short passage with attribution, the way you would quote any novel. Review, recommend, or criticise, including harshly. Make fan art or fan fiction for your own enjoyment and share it freely — just do not sell it or claim it as canon.

Print a chapter for your own reading, or save a page for offline reading on your own device. This is personal use.

Feeding it to a model to build a competing work, or to produce text in another author's voice. Using the sigil, the book titles, or the author name to promote anything else. Translations and audiobooks, which are rights I may grant — ask.

Why this page exists

Not to scare anyone. A rights page that forbids everything is ignored, and one that says nothing is used as evidence of indifference later. This is the middle: generous where generosity costs nothing, firm where the work would actually be harmed.
