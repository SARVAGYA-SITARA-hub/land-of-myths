Killer Petals

A novel by Sarvagya Sitara

Santino is a prince who was never wanted and is killed for it. He wakes on the altar he died on with a rose drinking his blood, and no memory of a bargain he does not remember making. Every death it feeds on turns another petal red.

A pot, an earthen pot. Anew which lay a moment ago, then broken in pieces on groundAnother appeared anew beside it-And yet when I saw another, then another and so on came in front of me, each differing in their demeanour…….And yet when I held my gaze, a rose appeared out of nowhere beneath the broken pot, the rose grew and grew in no time taking the pieces of broken pot and covering itself as to recreate what hath been destroyed.And yet when he made me wonder, went forth its roots devouring the vases which were anew withal he took a piece of each vase adding another to the vase he wore akin to a cloak.Cared nought the rose for he devoured when it smote the vases different.Weeping sung whence it went, leaving only annihilation in it’s wake.The vision on the altar, as it is written in the second chapter.
