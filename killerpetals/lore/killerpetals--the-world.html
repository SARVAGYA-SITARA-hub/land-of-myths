The "three seas"

Ostrand says "How in the great three seas is this possible." That implies a known set of three seas, of which the Estmark is presumably one. UNRESOLVED which the others are.

Politics

CONFIRMED from Aldric's memory: "All the countries of the eastern continent are now in our grasp... their country's ruler swapped with our people."

That is a specific accusation: not conquest, but replacement. Rulers swapped for agents. Whether it is true, a lie inside a memory, or the raving of a compromised man is the question in 02 Open Decisions #2.

IMPLIED: if it is true, then the empire's method is not war. It is substitution — and Santino is one of the few people in the world positioned to notice, because he is already supposed to be dead.

Calendar

  • "The third month" — winter, since the reservoir is iced.
  • No year, era, or dating system is given.

Seasons and weather

Monsoon-like rain at the cave. "The sun going down behind it in that fat, unhurried way." Wet cliffs, cold wind, humid underground air smelling "of smoke and tallow and something green." The world is wet and overcast more often than not.
