The Crew

Status: A working fishing boat. The first place he has ever been anonymous.

Confirmed

  • A fishing vessel under Capitan Ostrand, large enough for a deck crew, a main hatch,

and sails that can be let go all at once.

  • Crew traits: they howl in unison, argue about headings, go up the ropes, and *"the deck

came alive with men who were suddenly all in the way of each other and none of them in the way of the ship, which is the only skill that matters at sea."*

  • A former rope-maker's apprentice among them, who reads Santino's wrists: *"Two days.

Maybe three. They used the good stuff too — see how it bites."*

  • They check their eyes elsewhere when Ostrand collapses — this has happened before.
  • Nettle is first mate. Ostrand captains.
  • The ship carries a small boat with two oars and no sail.
  • There is a convoy — Ostrand is loud enough to be heard "on the other ship of a convoy."

The route

CONFIRMED: they were supposed to be on a different route. "when the black cats cut the route you're meant to be taking, you turn back." Ostrand did not turn back. "I should've listened."

UNRESOLVED: what "the black cats" are. A hazard, a faction, a sign, or weather. Thread: 02 Open Decisions.

What they are not

They are not a navy, not a trading company, and not interested in politics. They are working people who found something in their net. That is why Santino can be nobody here — and why this is the first place in the book where he is safe.

Names

Only Ostrand and Nettle are named. The rope-maker's apprentice and the rest are unnamed and should stay so until the manuscript names them.
