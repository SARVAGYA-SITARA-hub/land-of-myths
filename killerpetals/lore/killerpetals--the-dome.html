The Dome

Status: The temple where he died. Built for permanence, by someone who expected stalling.

Confirmed

"The dome was the roof of a world that had been put underground. Circular paths spiralled the walls, one above another, stairs running between them, and everywhere tunnel mouths opened off into dark — more passages than I could count, all of them converging on the floor. It was built the way a thing is built when whoever built it expected to be underground for a long time."

Details:

  • Circular layout. Spiral paths up the walls, stairs between levels.
  • Uncountable tunnels opening off the walls, all converging on the floor.
  • Air: warm, wet, smelled of smoke, tallow, "and something green." Under it a smell

he "could not place and did not like."

  • Acoustics: drums started *"from maybe twenty places at once and settling into one

rhythm, the way a crowd will find a heartbeat."*

  • A river above it — heard through the rock when the drums stopped.
  • Lit by fire, not daylight. No exit to the surface visible from the floor.
  • Above, in the forest, the entrance is a sinkhole — the ground *"went down and the

sound changed — a great hollowness."*

The altar

  • Grey, ancient stone, cracked "from top to bottom."
  • Green with something growing in the cracks *"a long time before the tribe or the

tunnel or the kingdom above us."*

  • Hung with offerings: garlands ("dried, some of them, and some not"), fruit, skins,

"small bundles of something I chose not to identify."

  • "The stone had the look of a thing that had received."
  • The surface he is forced onto is "a platform of rough stone."

What happened here, in order

  1. Santino is carried in, still bound, and dumped on his knees.
  2. Untied, then dressed in branch-cloth, then bound again behind his back.
  3. Dragged to the stone. The hymn. The rose moves.
  4. Kord brings the sword. The old man's hand rises, the drums stop.
  5. Beheading. Blood. "the whole dome turning the colour of the inside of a mouth."
  6. Time passes. Two days minimum — the stone is dry, and the tribe has taken everything.
  7. He wakes on the floor. Empty. The rose is gone. Kord's sword remains.
  8. He walks out. Two days to reach the coast.

What is missing when he wakes

CONFIRMED absent: the tribe, the drums, the hierophant, the rose, all the blood, the offerings, and any explanation.

CONFIRMED present: his body, his branch clothes, the rope marks on his wrists, the sword, the altar, a corpse he declines to describe.

"Somebody had been here and tidied."

IMPLIED: whoever tidied left the sword and took the rose. Either the tribe moved everything, or something else did.
