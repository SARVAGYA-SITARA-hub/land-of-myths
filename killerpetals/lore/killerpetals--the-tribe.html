The Tribe

Status: The congregation that killed him. Not soldiers, and that is the frightening part.

Confirmed

Appearance:

  • Hides over one shoulder and nothing over the other.
  • Arms painted wrist to elbow in an unknown pattern — *"lines and dots, like a road map

of somewhere I had never been."*

  • Bone through the ear. A cord of small teeth around the throat — enough that

Santino "made myself stop counting."

  • They carry prisoners on a carrying pole, tied wrists and ankles, upside down.

Behaviour:

  • They shout in a call-and-response: "HOLA!" answered by "ULA ULA ULA!"
  • They do not look at their prisoner. *"Nobody looked at me. Not once, not even the ones

carrying me — the pole was just a thing they were doing with their shoulders."*

  • They have practised liturgy: drums in twenty places, a hymn in an old language, a

masked celebrant, a fixed order of events.

  • They feed the stone, regularly and long-term.
  • They dress the sacrifice in branch-cloth — made of *"small branches bound together

with something flexible and alive that was still moving."*

  • They make him drink something "warm and thick" before the rite.
  • They took everything and left the sword.

The alarm in chapter 1

"They were not soldiers. That was the first thing I understood and the least useful."

IMPLIED: Santino expected soldiers because the men hunting him were hired blades. The tribe is not part of that hunt. He was taken by something entirely separate, which means his execution may not have been ordered by his father at all.

UNRESOLVED: who told them to take him. Or whether anyone did.

Their language

Fragments only: "Ola, hula huh u" from the hierophant. No glossary exists. Do not construct one — the text gives nothing to build on.

Whether they worship the rose

CONFIRMED: they bring offerings to the stone the rose sits on. UNRESOLVED: whether the rose is what they think it is, or whether they have been feeding something that merely resembles it. The author's own note says the anti-deity is "not evil, only negative" — see The Sun Symbol.

Where they are now

Gone. They took the rose, the offerings, and the drums, and left the dome empty and dry. They are still out there and they have the rose.
