[Getting Admission in Institute of Inter World Studies shifts Host's Narrative Impact to a global scale].

1. Art

[Agreed]

“Once more,” I said.

The wheel spun again.

This time, it landed on Art.

[Reward: Seed-Forging Art – Incomplete]

[Description: A method of forming ‘seeds’ within the body—condensed from elemental energy, similar to energy cores.]

[Limitations: Seeds can only hold non-terrestrial elemental energies. Earth-origin elemental flows cannot be contained.]

[Seeds will exhaust 3 days after being created]

[Balance: 5,100 Karmic coins]

I stared.

“Seeds,” I said. “Inside my body.”

[Please do not say it like that,] the Archive said. [You are making my log uncomfortable.]

“Why the limitation?” I asked. “No Earth elements?”

[You are, ironically, ‘over-subscribed’ to Earth already,] it said. [Your damaged lungs, your frail constitution, all highly terrestrial problems. The art is built to store foreign or cosmic energies. Think of it as… diversity.]

I considered the description.

“Any immediate use?” I asked.

[Not yet,] it said. [You are not currently bathing in alien elemental fields, unless this airline has drastically updated their service offerings.]

I snorted softly.

[ Again?] the Archive prompted.

I checked my balance.

[5,100 Karmic coins]

"Yes" I signalled

But to my surprise it landed on the very small part covered by ????

Suddenly the world came to a halt. My eyes blurred and my hair stood.

I tried to jump out of my seat but as if some invisible chains were wrapped around me and I felt a searing sensation in my chest as if someone has peeled a big chunk of myself and filled it with something..........sinister.

The Archive Window appeared in front of me, devoid of any playfulness and pitch black displaying in bold letters

[CURSE OF DESPAIR OBTAINED]

Description: You are someone who is bound to face hardships but that doesn’t only make you stronger but reveal something darker about you. Whenever you face any hardship and are pushed to boundaries your darker nature intensifies, It feeds on your sanity and magnifies the one negative emotion predominant in your character.

With that, I lost consciousness.

"Sir?" A hand shook me gently awake.

I opened my eyes to find the flight attendant looking down at me, worried.

"Sir, are you all right?" she asked. "You've been asleep for ten hours straight."

I checked myself over — nothing obviously wrong — then asked, as seriously as I could manage, "Can I get some water and food?"

She blinked, clearly not expecting that particular answer, then nodded and went to fetch some.

I stretched, wincing at the crooked angle I'd apparently slept in, and straightened myself in the seat.

"What was that?" I asked quietly. "Hey. You there?"

A moment passed before the translucent rectangle finally appeared.

[This is why elders forbid minors from gambling,] the Archive said.

"That's not the important part right now, is it?" I said.

[........] it kept it's silence

Seriously? I wanted to say — but the attendant chose that exact moment to return.

"Here. This is for you, sir." She set the tray down in front of me.

I ate.

[You look like a bear tearing apart its prey,] the Archive mused.

"Shuddup," I said, glaring at it through a full mouth.

Once I'd properly demolished the meal, I sat back and turned to the window. Below, city lights scattered like loose LEDs, the terrain sliding past at a speed that didn't feel real.

"When do we reach Lyon?" I asked.

[Two hours,] the Archive said.

I sat with that for a while, letting my thoughts settle into order.

Then I said, "Open Seed Forging Art."

The translucent window opened in front of me

My current focus now was to become stronger.

So I started editing the seed forging Art.

The interface changed

[Seed-Forging Art - Incomplete.]

[Description:] A method for forming ‘seeds’ within the body—condensed from elemental energy, similar to energy cores.[]

Mastery: 0%

(0 / 5)

"Non- Terrestial elemental energies, so that means I can contain fire of the Sun?" I questioned excitedly.

[Contextually: yes, Practically: Do not even try ] Archive advised

"Tch!" I ran my mind.

The interface in front of me changed according to my thoughts

[Description]

Form ===> Transform [200 Karmic Coins]

[Limitation]

[Net: 7,300 – 3,500 (Nocturnal + Intense physical exertion condition) = 3,800]

[Splendid, you totally covered the subconscious pulling of stellar energy by the cells with the excuse of making art seem like it is just for preparation of pulling stellar energy while masterfully speeding up the process with the excuse of Nocturnal time period and physical exertion] Archive observed.

I checked the changes closely

[What will you spend remaining coins on?] the Archive asked

"Null Craft" I said while thinking. The land was no longer visible as clouds embraced the earth like a shroud.

The interface for Null Craft opened

Null Craft
