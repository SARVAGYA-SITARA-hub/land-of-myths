Seed-Forging Art

Arins first Art, won from the Wheel of Fortune in ch 22 and immediately rewritten via EDIT.

As received

  • Method: forming seeds within the body, condensed from elemental energy — similar to energy cores.
  • Limitation: seeds can only hold non-terrestrial elemental energies. Earth-origin flows cannot be contained.
  • Decay: seeds exhaust three days after creation.
  • Method: breathing in an environment full of a given energy creates seeds of that type.
  • Mastery: 0% (0/5)

The Archive explains the limitation with the best line in the chapter:

[You are, ironically, oversubscribed to Earth already. Your damaged lungs, your frail constitution, all highly terrestrial problems.]

As rewritten — Art of Stellar Vessel

ChangedTo
FormTransform
SeedsCells
Condensed from elemental energyAntennas absorbing Stellar energy subconsciously
Breathing in a charged environmentAny physical activity begins the process

Plus a purchased limitation: the transformation happens only at night, and speeds up in powers of two with intense exertion.

Net cost 3,800 Karmic Coins. Mastery: 1/5.

Why the rewrite matters

He turned a passive, environment-dependent art into a subconscious, always-on, exertion-powered one — by paying for a restriction. The night-only clause is the admission ticket, and he hides an acceleration under it.

That is a design signature: Arin does not take the deal on offer, he argues it down and re-drafts it. It should recur every time he gains something.

Open questions

  • What are the five mastery tiers, and what does 5/5 look like?
  • Do cells persist forever, or decay like seeds did?
  • Does absorbing Stellar energy have a cumulative cost on his body?
