Nazarana Kingdom

574 words

History

  • Founded at the same time as Aurorian Empire.
  • Known as 'Land of Kuru' in the era of war.
  • Founded by a Council of Merchants and overtime evolved into a hierarchial structure.
  • Governance is not directed by nobles but the governors of 4 Major cities overseeing different branches of bussiness.
  • 'Tilmis' of the North did scholarly bussiness and postal duties throughout the continent as well as recording all the major incidents on the continent in their famous 'World History Tree'.
  • 'Ganj' of the east did bussiness of ores and minerals, often famous for its accurate appraisals on rare minerals. It was also one of the great exporter of copper and granite.
  • 'Baazar' of the west didn't had any speciality yet thrived from tourism all over the world due to being the host of largest market. It is said that the things you cannot find anywhere will definitely be found here.
  • Tourists, rich men, women and merchants come here all year around to buy and sell goods.
  • Lastly comes 'Somalis' of the south, it specialized in information bussiness and mainly lived off mercenary, labour bussiness with a couple of hidden organizations which offered information for a reasonable price.
  • All governors have their private army, but due to 'Kalman's Oath' they cannot directly attack and usurp the crown since Kalman's oath gives the royal family and their lineage absolute right over the land of Kuru, but on the other hand it is due to that the governors also have their positions solidified, since it also states that the crown cannot appoint anyone the ruler of any of the four major cities outside of the current governor's direct lineage.
  • Kalman's oath when broken will curse that lineage forever and no one from that lineage shall receive the blessings of spirits for years to come.
  • The Kalman's oath is said to be overseen by Azevalgar (Protector of Kuru). Even the pale men seems to know about Kalman's oath.
  • The oath does NOT bind the Order of Zenos — Azevalgar created the Order, so the oath cannot harm them. Zenos may betray any governor or the crown with no backlash at all. Full mechanic in Kalman's Oath.
