History

The land previously was known as the land of 'Sama'. In the Era of war there were no kingdoms established and civilizations ran amok. People lived in groups and no order existed.

At that time there was a shrine, an empty shrine at the heart of this land where now stood the royal castle after 600 years. In that empty shrine resided a blind man. He may have been blind but his ears, nose, touch and intuition were so great that he could live like a normal person.

At that time there was no order and so to protect himself from bandits. He developed a method, he firmly believed that a human was never meant to just crawl on the land and there must have been something which he can accomplish and so through arduous exercises and means compiled with his terrifying control over his senses he managed to successfully develop a method.

He writes in the text which is the only known text from that era. "I pass on this method to whoever reads this, By concentrating on your breath, take in the air and feel it going through your nerves, next guide the coolness spreading in your chest towards your legs, contracting them and releasing moment after all the while holding your nerves in place, speeding up circulation in bursts. This shall ultimately give you mastery over 'Speed'."

This is the only letter and along with it are just his diary about the chaotic life of those days. However at the end of that diary there is an interesting entry. "They are coming, many of them, they told me to close the shrine. But, How do I put it? They don't seem.....alive. Their voices are like a hiss of a snake and their touch, it is cold."
