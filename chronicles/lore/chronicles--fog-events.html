Fog Events

status: proposed. Named in the published text (ch 1, as Fog Phenomenology; ch 33, titled Fog (1)). Mechanism unresolved.

What they are

Provisional zones. Inside a fog, reality is under review — legible to an editor, amendable, and not yet settled. Ordinary rules hold, but they hold loosely.

The published text treats Fog as an academic subject: "Fog Phenomenology" is examined in the aptitude paper. That is a superb choice — it means the existence of fogs is common knowledge, treated as curriculum, and therefore banal. Nobody is frightened of the thing that will kill them.

Why they exist

Proposed: a review in progress. See Author Candidacy. Where the manuscript is being examined, the paper goes thin — that thinness is fog.

What they do

  • Make worlds amendable. Edits land more easily inside a fog.
  • Make people unrecorded. The same property that lets Arin forge lets a candidate vanish.
  • Gate promotion. Passing review inside a fog is the proposed route to Candidacy.

Eldoris

00 Eldoris Overview is a fog world — the preliminary test, per the author. Pass and return to Earth.

Open questions

  • Are fogs natural, engineered, or deliberate examination halls?
  • How long does one last? Days, centuries, subjective years?
  • What happens to the people inside a fog that fails review?
  • Can a fog be entered deliberately? If yes, by whom?
