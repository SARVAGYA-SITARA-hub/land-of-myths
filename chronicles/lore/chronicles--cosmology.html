Cosmology

status: proposed — imported from the master bible. Not canon until the author promotes it.

Core premise

The multiverse is a manuscript under review. Reality can be written, interpreted, divided, and rewritten.

Existence is not a machine and not a dream. It is a draft under editorial review. Worlds are submitted, examined, found wanting or sufficient, and either permitted to stand or revised out of existence.

Structure of reality

Ordinary reality

One Earth, one life, cause and effect. Ordinary people are unaware they are inside a manuscript at all.

Supernatural reality

The three Occult Substrates — the media a manuscript can be written in. Fortune, weather, faith, myth, rumour and memory are not flavour; they are the ink. See Spiritual Energy of Shunya, Primordial Qi of Chaos, Veil of Maya.

Higher-order reality

Above the substrates sit The Editors and the reviewing apparatus. Reality is legible to them the way a page is legible to a reader.

The Manuscript

For reality to be a manuscript means it has an author, a version, a margin for error, and a reader. It also means it can be plagiarised — which is precisely what Arin Valen does.

The laws that follow

  1. A claim must be justified. Anything written into reality must be paid for. This is why Mythology and Rumor costs Arin something every time it works.
  2. Belief is fuel. A thing believed in becomes load-bearing, whether or not it is true. This is the engine of The False Prophet.
  3. Division is a valid edit. Reality can be split rather than rewritten — hence Divine vs Undivine as two alignments, not good vs evil.

Why the antagonists work

An Editor is not a villain. An Editor has a standard, and Arin fails it. The horror is procedural: he is not being hunted, he is being reviewed.

Open questions

  • Does the manuscript have one author or a committee?
  • Is Earth an original or a copy? The published premise — "the same Earth you died. But it is not exactly the same" — implies a copy, which implies a forger.
  • What happens to a world after review fails?
  • Can a world refuse review? That may be what Primordial Qi of Chaos is for.
