EDIT

The Archive's paid rewriting function. Arin does not merely receive rewards — he alters their wording before accepting them.

How it works (ch 22, in full)

He takes Seed-Forging Art — Incomplete and buys three changes:

ChangeCost
Form → Transform200
Seeds → Cells600
[condensed from elemental energy] → [into antennas capable of absorbing Stellar energy subconsciously]1,500
Subtotal2,300

Then he buys a limitation to reduce the price — cell transformation only at night, speeding up in powers of two with intense exertion. The Archive prices the restriction at −3,500, and he also folds in a benefit under the same clause. Total: 3,800.

Why this is the best mechanical idea in the system

The EDIT function lets him pay to be clever. Limitations reduce cost, so the optimal move is to design a narrow, strange, personal technique rather than a broad one. It rewards exactly the trait Arin has — unwillingness to accept the given wording — and it means his powers are engineered, which is far more interesting than granted.

It also gives the Archive its best comedy, because it is an accountant watching a teenager commit small frauds:

[This should be illegal] [Splendid, you totally covered the subconscious pulling of stellar energy…]

Note for the prose

The Archive objects, prices, and complies. It is never enthusiastic and never obstructive. Keep it as a clerk, not a conscience.

Open questions

  • Are there limits on what can be edited — can a reward's existence be rewritten?
  • Can a curse be edited? Nobody has tried.
