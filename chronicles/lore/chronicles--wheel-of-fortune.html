Wheel of Fortune

status: canon — used on the page in ch 22 and ch 32.

What it is

A four-segment gamble bought with Karmic Coins. You spin, you get what you get.

SegmentReward
1Art — a technique or discipline
2Skill — a discrete ability
3Artifact — an object
4???? — appeared in ch 22; it was not there before

Costs and rewards, as paid

  • Ch 22 balance: 5,300 coins (1,000 written exam + 4,000 interview + 300)
  • Spin 1 → Skill: Omnilingual (5 m radius, spoken and unspoken)
  • Spin 2 → Art: Seed-Forging Art — Incomplete
  • Spin 3 → ???? → Curse of Despair
  • Remaining after edits: 1,200

The fourth segment

This is the one that matters. Arin notices it before he spins — "'???? wasn't there before,' I noted" — and spins anyway, because the Archive's warning was cut off mid-word.

The wheel is not safe and never was. The presence of an unlabelled segment is the whole warning, and he read it as an opportunity. That should colour every future spin.

Editing

The Archive allows EDIT — buying alterations to a reward's wording before accepting it. Arin turned Seed-Forging Art into Art of Stellar Vessel this way, converting seeds into cells and breathing into any physical activity, at a net cost of 3,800 coins.

This is his most distinctive use of the system: not drawing good rewards, but rewriting mediocre ones. It is the behaviour of a man who cannot leave a thing alone, and it is the same trait that fills Cheonma's blanks in another story entirely — here it fills the Archive's small print.

Open questions

  • Does segment four always produce a curse, or only sometimes?
  • Can a curse be edited like an Art can? (Nobody has tried. Someone should.)
  • Can the wheel be spun for someone else?
