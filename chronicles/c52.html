Fugitive [3]

1,160 words
Reading on

[Vazir’s POV]

The words crashed down like a divine judgement.

I turned around and saw a fat man who was none other than the chief accountant along with some guards and a lean man beside him staring daggers at the chancellor.

“What is the meaning of this?” I asked turning towards the fat man. Chancellor too seemed to have seen a ghost as he opened his mouth trying to say something and only to close it right after.

The lean man stepped forward, he bowed and said “Your highness, we have found records of embezzlements from the royal treasury. The records are not in proper shape but nonetheless the proof is concrete”.

He handed me a bunch of papers which bore the official seal of the kingdom.

I more I delved into the paper and its contents the more my breathing quickened. It was a record, a record of total expenditure from the royal treasury. On surface everything seemed to be alright but if someone were to calculate the numbers, they would catch it. The calculation was amiss, by a very thin margin in every column.

I scanned it from top to bottom, no matter how much I wanted to refute it, the proof was right in front of me.

I lifted my gaze with a twisted face. My left brow twitched involuntarily

‘Old man Wallabh? Out of all people?’ unlike any other personnel I had known Wallabh since childhood. He was practically my first teacher. I could still vividly remember his big hands covering mine and teaching me to write alphabet by alphabet.

I was about to question him when-

“Your highness, I swear upon my honor it wasn’t me!” he pleaded, his hands trembled. He went down on his knees, grabbed my left arm with his palms.

“Those papers, they are fabricated!” his grip on my arm tightened.

“Enough! Guards take him” the chief accountant commanded.

“Y-your highness, you have…no please believe me I beg of you” he came closer, now grabbing my arm like a tree branch to prevent his fall.

“Blasphemous!, this is an insult to the crown!” the lean man yelled.

Seeing the chancellor hide behind me the soldiers stopped, confused.

For a moment silence settled inside the room.

“Guards!” the soldiers straightened hearing my voice.

I looked towards old man Wallabh, his face was pitiful.

“Throw him in the cell” I said.

Chancellor’s face paled but the soldiers instantly complied, their steel armor clanking in unison as they held Wallabh’s arms whose grip felt like clay.

He didn’t make any commotion, his eyes were wide open and jaw hanging as he couldn’t process what had happened while soldiers dragged him out of the room.

I closed my eyes and sighed. “You” I said turning my head towards the lean man.

He turned towards me and bowed “Your highness?”

“I haven’t seen you before, present your credentials in the office.” at my words he didn’t speak which was same as affirmation.

“….And you” I said turning towards chief accountant.

He bowed slightly “Your Highness?” he asked.

“Arrange for someone to act as temporary chancellor and…” I thought for a moment before continuing.

“Connect me with the governors through magician’s glass, until I go get some fresh air”. At that both of them ran out to make the preparations.

I too stepped out of the inner castle and in the garden, it reminded me of my sister. I touched the petals of roses as I walked along the edge of inner walls and at some point I slipped inside a watch tower quietly.

There were 4 towers, one in a direction each and two ways to go inside a watch tower which were built within the inner walls.

One way was through the wall itself, the way on the top of walls where soldiers patrolled was connected to the tower and one can descend inside the tower from the door there.

One was through a trapdoor hidden in the shade of roses and leaves. I slid into the hole. Descending the ladder, insides were dark with a thick and oily smell. My eyes turned orange from its original lavender color and sparks appeared crackling in my left hand.

Illuminating the room I saw spider webs and layers of dust present over the old furniture.

“Sadly no one is aware of such a nice place” I lamented. This place hadn’t been looked after for years. It used to be my hideout when I was young. I saw a painting of myself and sister hung on the far right of the room. I stepped on the stairs in the middle of the room which led to the next floor.

My steps echoed as I finally stopped in front of an old drawer. This room was clean unlike the lower room. I pulled the handle.

Its joints screeched as the old drawer revealed a diary.

“I see….-“ a smile made its way up to my lips.

“You are still sharp as ever, Wallabh” I murmured to myself.

I closed my eyes and recalled the exchange that happened a few moments prior.

“Your highness, I swear upon my honor it wasn’t me!” he pleaded, his hands trembled. He went down on his knees, grabbed my left arm with his palms.

His finger tapped twice on my knuckles. (Inner castle)

“Those papers, they are fabricated!” his grip on my arm tightened.

Then he made three circles on my wrist (Third Watchtower)

“Enough! Guards take him” the chief accountant commanded.

Two taps (Second Floor) and an arrow facing upwards (From below)

“Y-your highness, you have…no please believe me I beg of you” he came closer, now grabbing my arm like a tree branch to prevent his fall.

Then he gave a slight pinch to my elbow (Emergency!)

“Using a game to convey such a message? I expected no less from you old man” I whispered to the walls.

Then opened my eyes and in the darkness which dared not shroud my gaze from the truth, I opened the diary.

I turned my head and saw a few griffins above. “Hayato san, into the forest!” I screamed as I steered the horse towards the nearest opening in the forest.
