Vessel of Stars (II)

1,135 words
Reading on

[Dara's POV]

"When he saw salamander behind him he ran like crazy" said Tanni

"Pfft- what" one of my group members laughed while spilling his ale on the ground trying to contains his laughter.

The glowing stones filled the room with a dim orange glow. Fire place kept the room warm.

My fellow group members were wasted on jugs of ale and plates of crisp potato slices.

I too listened to stories, our former captain or my dear friend and mentor Tanni told us maybe twentieth time, but hey in between friends the time spent matters not the bullshit going on the table.

After sometime when all of them fell asleep on the table I slowly got up, took the plates and was about to move to kitchen when the door of my room opened and my younger brother entered crying.

I turned my head and saw not his face but his arm which was twisted in the wrong direction, turning purple in color.

CRASH!

The plates fell from my hand, producing a loud shattering noise and waking up everyone.

"W-wha happened?" Tanni instantly grabbed the handle of his sword.

Then he saw me and turned his head, his eyes widened "How did this happen?" he stood up.

Now everyone turned towards him and took him to the table and asked.

He began to recount everything, my knuckles tightened as time passed.

"All of you get ready" my blood was boiling. I was about to march straight to that inn and kill that son of a bitch.

"Calm down for now-" Tanni put his hand on my shoulder.

"HOW AM I SUPPOSED TO-" I tried to argue.

"If I heard it right, then he is not any normal bastard" Tanni advised me.

I took a deep breath and put my anger aside for a moment.

Meanwhile the other guys took my younger brother inside and treated his injury.

"What do we do then?" I asked Tanni.

Tanni smiled and began to unfold the masterful plan he had come with.

I had taken care of my younger brother since he was eight years old, A bit behind normal people in terms of brains but my only relative.

Our father, a local mercenary died when I was just twelve and two years after that, mom ran away with another man.

It was at fifteen years of age that I began to notice some weird things.

I could feel the wind, not through senses but rather could see through it, could feel it becoming one with me.

The leader of a famous mercenary group and my mentor Tanni found me when I fought with one of his group members over a meagre sum of 20 copper coins and absolutely overpowered him.

That's when he took me as an apprentice and began to nurture my talent.

"You are blessed by spirits" Tanni told me about my weird experience.

"Nonethless the spirit of wind" He smiled.

Soon I surpassed everyone in our group and made a name for myself in principality and lands far and wide.

Money was there, friends were there, a good life was there but my brother who was to serve at the frostgate hold had to go there for 5 years as a mandatory service after turning 16.

When he came back safe and sound I was overjoyed and threw a party.

The life began to get on the track until today when he came back crying and an arm ruined.

The plan was to silently follow them till the meadows outside the border appeared and to launch an ambush, kill them all and take care of corpses and then drag the girl to my younger brother's bed.

Like that there will be no consequences since those meadows were an unclaimed territory and the wet grass will act as a good cover to hide corpses. A royal carriage would fetch a decent price even from a small kingdom like of Parshara's.

This was the plan until now that is

THWACK! CRUNCH!

The feeling of lower jaw vanished and only pain remained, I lost the grip on my great sword flying for a couple of meters.

I quickly stood up adjusting my posture.

The blessing of wind spirit enveloped me and physical world dissolved

The world slowed, feeling of senses expanding and in that field only for a fraction of second I felt a presence at the edge and the next moment that presence was in front of me.

I raised my arm to block whatever was coming.

WHACK!

A kick on my left upper arm, but the force was so terrible that the sheer strength sent me flying off my feet.

I hadn't even touched the ground but the presence was already upon me. I crossed my arms to block.

But instead the presence grabbed my leg and slammed me into the ground.

BOOM!

"khaarggghh" a wet gargle escaped my mouth and stars began to dance in my vision.

I sat upright my hands trembling and saw, two grey eyes. The man whom my brother should have killed already stood before me. A strange white grey glow enveloped him.

I have seen red, blue, green and brown glow countless of times, but a white glow was a first.

'The blessings of a spirit king?' the thought surfaced but was quickly rejected.

If it would have been a spirit king I would still have been at least able to put up a fight, but I was overpowered by a large margin so whatever that is.

Is on par with the one blessed by a spirit king.

I was thinking but just then something came bouncing in my lap.

I felt something warm and wet in my hands.

It was the head of my younger brother. He looked scared.

My mind blanked, grief and madness overran my thoughts.

"NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO" I screamed.

I stood up and began my final act as a big brother.

Wind began to cover me.

"I'LL KILLLL YOOUUUUUUUUUUUU" my nerves swelled as I screamed.

Without caring for my body I jumped.

I punched

BOOM!

His feet trembled.

I gave a jab as a feint and then kneed his abdomen.

He jumped backward trying to lessen the impact.

'An opening' I thought and pushed in inside.

"Don't" I heard Tanni scream but I believed in myself and stepped in front of him my hand blade ready to hear him apart.

The moment I moved my hand blade.

He looked up and smiled

"Got you" he whispered.

Before however I could understand his palms striked the sides of my head.

My eardrums ruptured.

"Shit" I cursed while my sense of direction collapsed and a moment after that I lost the feeling of my torso as my vision spun.

'I am sorry little bro, I couldn't take your revenge'
