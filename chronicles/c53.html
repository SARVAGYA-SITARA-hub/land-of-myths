Guerilla Warfare

1,064 words
Reading on

Authors Note: After a lot of thought I have arrived at a conclusion that from now on the chapters will be in MULTI POV form. (One POV per chapter)

I at first tried to steer the narrative in 3rd person camera locked at the current scene form but my brain asked “Can you accept such an Outcome?” and my heart answered “No”

For some reason I felt that this is a reasonable development, what are your thoughts?

*

{Arin’s POV}

I steered the horse towards the nearest opening of the forest. Along with it my eyes turned gray, and the world slowed down.

As I entered the forest, the sounds of birds chirping and leaves rustling entered my ear. Shade of the trees covered my figure preventing the armored soldiers from spotting me and I heard the sound wing coming closer, turned my head and my prediction proved to be right.

The Aerial soldiers pursuing us abandoned the advantage of their position and came closer, flying just over the trees to spot me and Hayato san.

I jumped towards a thick tree branch letting the horse run ahead and turned around. The world slowed down as and vision turned blue as if submerged in water. I saw three griffins all of them near the trees.

‘Either do it in one swoop or fast enough that no one gets to react’ I naturally reached a conclusion.

The surface of the tree branch cracked and I launched towards the nearest pursuer from the vast expanse of leaves and silence.

“Whe-?”his head turned slowly like a film set on 0.5x speed and his hands seemed to slowly raise trying to protect himself, but I was faster.

“Khugh..” he shrieked as my knee slammed onto the cap of his nose. I felt something crack where I had hit him. The force however sent him flying off the griffin and on the ground somewhere in the forest.

I turned my gaze on my right where the remaining two were, however they had their guard up.

I ran on air with null craft in a zig zag motion so fast that their eyes couldn’t follow and pounced on the left one while going in the direction of the right one.

He couldn’t even understand anything before my horse kick shattered his ribs causing a nasty dent and the inertia, true to its word sent the rider flying in the green sea below.

I turned back and saw the last one putting a metal pipe in his mouth. I ran towards him but till then he had already blown the whistle.

He swung his already drawn out sword but I summer saulted and grabbed his head pushing him off the griffin.

The griffins without their riders became confused and began to fly in a circle behind each other’s tails. I tried to control a griffin yet, it seemed they had some basic intelligence which allowed them to identify me and hence I got a clear rejection whenever I tried to approach them.

I sighed and dashed down into the forest searching for Hayato san. I didn’t had to search for long before I found his horse after some time and him lying upside down on the ground.

“Hayato san!” I yelled his name trying to wake him up. His face looked thinner than before. I frowned and looked around, there was nothing, just a maze of trees and leaves.

“D-Daniel?” he mumbled my name opening his eyes. I opened my mouth to say something but closed it for some reason.

“Are you ok?” I asked in my western accent, a bit hesitant.

He nodded and then asked me “Did…we lose the pursuers?” he questioned in a low voice.

“Yes..we lost them” I answered.

“Where do we go now?” he asked.

I thought for a moment and then told him about the whistle.

“To my prediction If we both travel together now, we will we easily identified”

“So…you mean we-?”

“Yes…let’s split up and meet at Somalis in the south in a couple of days” I suggested.

“But if they find-?”

“No one will know” I told him and then led him to the soldiers I had defeated a couple of moments ago.

“You did this?” he seemed a bit surprised.

I started taking off their armor and put it on myself. Hayato san seeing me also starting wearing the armor of one of them. After we were done I took the two off whom we wore the armor and threw them somewhere deep in the forest giving last of my responsibility to the mother nature.

“How much time will it take you to reach Somalis?” He asked me.

“Two Days” I gave him a vague number.

“How should I contact you?” he asked.

“You don’t know?” I asked, lifting my brow.

He frowned and said “I know but, don’t you think the current situation demands us to switch to a more hidden means of communication?”

“Hmmm” I nodded while looking elsewhere.

**“Remain in Somalis for today and then tomorrow at night, I will set a forest fire. When you see the forest fire, start moving towards Velmor duchies”** I said

“Where will you remain till then?” He asked

“I told you didn’t I? I have a good friend in ‘Ganj’. He will help me set up our escape” I smiled. He nodded and hopped onto the back of the horse.

“Okay then, I will stay at a village near Somalis. Don’t be late” he gave a light kick to the horse and began moving towards Somalis.

I saw his figure vanishing and bit my lips.

‘Neither did I had any friend in Ganj and nor did we had any means of communication’ I thought.

I let out a long sigh, I would have really overlooked it hadn’t Hayato san talked to me in Japanese the whole tour and called me ‘Arin’ instead of ‘Daniel’.

Now, what should I do? Should I try to save him? I even left the soldiers and Hassan there to escape.

I turned my head and saw small dots in the sky, becoming bigger. Many of them, infact dozens.

“Let’s first get out of this situation. Logically, I cannot save them now. Let’s take one step….at a time”.
